# TalentTrivo — Project Context

## 1. Project Overview

TalentTrivo is a career transition and job-support business focused on helping experienced professionals move into new technology areas and secure employment.

TalentTrivo currently operates through a human-led agent/counselor model.

The agent:

1. Captures a lead.
2. Collects the candidate's current resume and professional details.
3. Reviews the candidate's current experience.
4. Helps identify a suitable target technology.
5. Explains the market opportunity when the candidate already has a target technology.
6. Helps prepare the candidate for the target technology.
7. Rewrites the candidate's resume for the target technology.
8. Helps prepare the candidate's Naukri and LinkedIn profiles.
9. Searches for relevant job opportunities.
10. Supports job applications.
11. Helps prepare the candidate for interviews.
12. Continues support until the candidate clears an interview and gets placed.

The long-term objective is to turn this manual operating model into a technology-enabled career transition platform.

---

## 2. Product Vision

TalentTrivo should become:

> **An AI-powered career transition and talent operations platform for experienced professionals.**

The platform should not initially try to replace the TalentTrivo agent.

Instead, the product should:

> **Digitize the agent's workflow → capture structured data → apply AI → automate repetitive work.**

The agent remains responsible for important decisions and candidate interactions while the platform provides structure, intelligence, automation and operational visibility.

## 5. Primary Users

### Visitor

A person visiting the public TalentTrivo website.

### Lead

A person who has expressed interest but has not yet completed the candidate onboarding process.

### Candidate / Job Seeker

A registered person whose career transition is being managed by TalentTrivo.

### Agent / Counselor

The primary operational user.

The agent manages candidate relationships and performs career-transition activities.

### Admin

Responsible for platform administration, agents, technologies, configuration and operational oversight.

### Future Employer / Recruiter

A possible future user type.

This should not be implemented in the initial phases unless explicitly added to the backlog.

## 8. Development Philosophy

TalentTrivo should be developed incrementally.

The product should follow:

```text
Manual Workflow
      ↓
Digital Workflow
      ↓
Structured Data
      ↓
AI Assistance
      ↓
Automation
      ↓
Intelligence Network
```

The initial product should prioritize operational visibility and reliable data capture over sophisticated AI.

---

## 9. Important Architectural Principles

### Application Is the System of Record

The TalentTrivo application/database is the source of truth for:

* Candidates
* Leads
* Jobs
* Companies
* Applications
* Interviews
* Status
* Activities
* Other business records

### n8n Is Orchestration

n8n may coordinate:

* AI workflows
* Notifications
* Data enrichment
* Job discovery
* External integrations
* Scheduled processing

n8n should not become the primary business database.

### AI Is Intelligence

AI should be used for tasks requiring:

* Interpretation
* Classification
* Analysis
* Generation
* Matching
* Summarization
* Recommendations to the agent

### External Platforms Are External Systems

Naukri, LinkedIn and other external platforms should be treated as external systems.

Their data should not be assumed to be the TalentTrivo system of record.

---

## 10. Human-in-the-Loop Principle

TalentTrivo should initially use AI as an assistant to the agent.

Important actions should allow human review.

Examples:

* Technology transition recommendation
* Resume changes
* Job matching
* Candidate communication
* Profile changes
* Job applications

Automation should increase gradually as reliability is demonstrated.

---

## 11. Current Product Strategy

The first objective is not to build the complete TalentTrivo vision.

The first objective is to build a reliable:

> **Agent Operations MVP**

The MVP should allow TalentTrivo agents to manage the complete early candidate journey digitally.

The system should establish the foundation required for later AI capabilities.
